Cross-Border Transfer Assessment

1 Transfer description
Record the exporter, recipient, data subjects, data categories, purpose, frequency, destination, storage period, and onward transfers.

2 Risk assessment
A transfer impact assessment must be completed before personal data is transferred overseas. The assessment considers local access laws, enforceability, technical safeguards, and practical experience.

3 Approval and review
Privacy approves the transfer mechanism and supplementary measures. Assessments are reviewed when the service, destination, recipient, or applicable law changes.
