Vendor Processing Standard

1 Due diligence
Privacy and Security assess the nature of processing, data categories, hosting locations, subcontractors, control evidence, and incident history before onboarding.

2 Contract controls
Processor agreements must include confidentiality, security, deletion, cooperation, audit, breach notification, and subprocessor duties. Material deviations require Privacy approval.

3 Ongoing oversight
Critical processors are reviewed annually. Control failures are assigned an owner and tracked to closure before contract renewal.
