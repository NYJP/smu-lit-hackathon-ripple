Data Subject Request Procedure

1 Intake and identity verification
Requests are logged on receipt. The service team verifies identity using information proportionate to the sensitivity of the requested records.

2 Search and collection
System owners search customer, support, billing, marketing, and archived repositories. Legal reviews proposed redactions for third-party information and privilege.

3 Response deadline
Data-subject access requests must be completed within 30 days from verified receipt. The case owner records pauses, clarification requests, and any lawful extension.

4 Delivery and audit trail
Responses are delivered through an approved secure channel. The request, searches, approvals, response package, and delivery confirmation are retained in the case record.
