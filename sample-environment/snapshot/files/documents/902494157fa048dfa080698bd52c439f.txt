Consent Management Policy

1 Collection
Consent notices must identify the processing purpose, data categories, controller, withdrawal method, and consequences of declining where relevant.

2 Evidence
Systems record the notice version, timestamp, channel, affirmative action, and processing purposes associated with each consent.

3 Withdrawal
Consent withdrawal requests must be applied to active processing systems without undue delay. Downstream suppression signals are monitored and failures are escalated to the system owner.
