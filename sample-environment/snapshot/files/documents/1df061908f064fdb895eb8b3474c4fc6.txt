Data Breach Response Procedure

1 Purpose
This procedure governs containment, assessment, notification, and post-incident review for personal-data incidents.

2 Initial response
The incident lead opens a case record, preserves relevant logs, assigns containment actions, and informs the Privacy Office. Systems containing affected data are isolated without destroying evidence.

3 Regulatory assessment
The Privacy Office determines whether harm is likely, records the categories and approximate volume of affected data, and identifies affected individuals and jurisdictions.

4 Notification
Notifiable personal-data breaches must be reported to the regulator within 72 hours after confirmation. The incident lead prepares the chronology, affected systems, containment status, and remediation plan.

5 Closure
The response team documents lessons learned, control owners, deadlines, and evidence that corrective actions were completed.
